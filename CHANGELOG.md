# Changelog


## 0.3.2 (2026-11-09)

- Entity overlay: four outline styles (3D box, 3D corners, 2D box, 2D corners), a fill with its own strength, and a picture on the page that shows the look as you change it.
- Entity overlay: pick the mobs to show from the whole Bestiary, 255 mobs sorted by place, with a search.
- New Toggle sprint: sprints for you while you walk forward.

## 0.3.1 (2026-11-09)

- Slot locking: an empty locked slot takes items again, and the line of a binding is drawn only after you hold the lock key for a second.
- Inventory buttons no longer show in dungeon terminals and in the Experimentation Table games.
- Item rarity colors now also show in the auction house, whose items list the seller and the bids after the rarity.
- Hide armor also hides skull helmets. The elytra option is gone, SkyBlock has none.
- Mention chime stays quiet for your own messages and for your name inside another word.
- Fairy Souls read the island and the profile from the tab list, so they show up on Hypixel.
- Entity overlay leaves out SkyBlock NPCs when Hide NPCs is on.
- Deployable timers match whole words only, skip mob health tags and have a range setting, in a new panel look.
- Profit tracker adds the coins in your purse, says when prices are unavailable and has a panel look.
- Chest value: the unpriced note no longer sticks out of the panel.
- Scrollable tooltips can move every tooltip with the wheel, not only tall ones. Turn it off in the settings if you prefer.
- The Array list matches the accent of your theme, with panels and a fade, and still has a color of its own to pick.
- The Inventory HUD lost its armor and offhand row.
- The hotbar's wells, selection and rarity colors sit exactly in the middle of each slot.
- Profile viewer: the setup text now says how to get a Hypixel development key.
- Entity overlay tags hang over the head and shrink with distance instead of growing as you walk away.
- The Infobar grows its update status in smoothly and gives back the room when the text gets shorter.

## 0.3.0 (2026-11-08)

- New Macros area: Experiment macro plays Chronomatron, Ultrasequencer and Superpairs for you, with a stats card, failsafes and optional renewals. Automation can get your account banned: it is off until you switch it on.
- New Auto clicker, Etherwarp target, Freecam, Trail line, Fullbright, Hide effects and Menu blur.
- New Copy chat (Ctrl + click a message), Peek chat and Mention chime. The Name changer now handles up to six names, each with a color, a gradient or a rainbow.
- New Profile viewer, Profit tracker and Deployable timers.
- Small features now share pages: Chat tools, Inventory tools, Camera tools and Visual tweaks, each feature with its own switch.
- One Panel background setting in Appearance for the scoreboard, info bar, chat and every other panel.
- Config profiles are managed right on their page, and never carry your API key.
- The account picture sits in the button row of the title screen.

## 0.2.1 (2026-11-07)

- A refreshed look: one corner style across cards, buttons and panels, a new Home dashboard, a command palette and a HUD editor with undo and snapping.
- Item rarity colors behind items in the hotbar, inventory and storage screens, with one switch in Appearance.
- Item Chams: a tint, an outline and a glow, each with its own strength setting.
- The Inventory HUD no longer covers an open inventory.
- Quiet interface sounds for click, switch, open and close, with optional hover sounds.

## 0.2.0 (2026-11-06)

- A new menu: features sorted into areas, one search for features and settings, and your pinned and recent features on Home.
- A new title screen with an Account manager and a Proxy manager for SOCKS5, SOCKS4 and HTTP proxies.
- New Inventory HUD: your inventory on the screen, with icons and stack sizes.
- New Hide armour and Player size: leave armour pieces out and scale the player model.
- New Entity overlay: boxes, tracers and tags for players and mobs.
- New Fairy Souls: marks known souls, shows the distance and keeps progress per profile.
- New Chest value: the Bazaar value of the storage page you have open, with a breakdown per item.
- New Aspect ratio: 4:3, 16:9, 16:10, 21:9 or a custom world aspect, stretched or letterboxed.
- New Crimson Isle dailies, Auto conversation and ATLAS navigation. They are off by default; see /crimson help and /atlas help.

## 0.1.11 (2026-11-03)

- New Pet display: the active pet with its icon, level, experience, held item and a ring in its rarity color.
- Pets of the same name are told apart now, so two pets with one name no longer get mixed up.
- The tab list's pet widget keeps the pet's experience live, if you switch it on in SkyBlock's tab settings.
- The equipment and pet panel in the inventory shows the rarity of each piece.
- Slot locker: armor slots can be bound to inventory and hotbar slots, and a slot can have several partners.
- Changelogs in the chat after an update, and with /requiem changelog.

## 0.1.10 (2026-11-02)

- New Zoom: hold C to zoom in, scroll to change how far.
- New Freelook: press V to look around without turning.
- New Item scale: size, position, rotation and swing of the item in your hand.
- New Infinite chat: scroll back through thousands of lines instead of 100.
- New Party buttons: Kick, Promote, Transfer and Disband in /pl.
- New Auction scroll: the mouse wheel turns the pages of the auction house.
- Fixed the ping in the Info HUD, the colors of item names above the hotbar and the alignment of the arrows in the menu.
- The module list in the menu scrolls now.
